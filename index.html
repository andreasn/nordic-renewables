Capacity: IRENA, Renewable Energy Statistics 2026 (published July 2026), via Our World in Data's copy of the series; wind is onshore plus offshore, solar is total solar. Norway's solar series starts later than the others.

2026–2030 outlook: wind from WindEurope, Wind energy in Europe: 2025 statistics and the outlook for 2026-2030 (February 2026). Its expected yearly onshore and offshore installations are added to IRENA's 2025 capacity, capped at WindEurope's stated 2030 totals where retirements make the yearly sum overshoot; WindEurope's own count starts about 1 GW higher for the Nordics, mostly in Sweden. Solar from DNV's February 2024 article on Nordic solar, which cites SolarPower Europe's 2023 outlook for Denmark, Sweden and Finland and DNV's own Energy Transition Outlook for Norway. Finland's solar figure is approximate: the source says capacity will almost triple from about 1 GW.

Generation: IRENA's generation table is only available through its query database, so this uses Eurostat's gross electricity production (dataset nrg_bal_peh, wind and solar PV), the official statistics IRENA's European figures draw on. Sweden's values match IRENA's country profile exactly; Denmark's recent solar output is higher in Eurostat's current release than in IRENA's profile, most likely from later revisions, so small gaps against IRENA are possible.

Data files: capacity (IRENA) · generation (Eurostat) · 2030 outlook · 2026–2030 projections. Sources: IRENA Renewable Energy Statistics 2026, Eurostat nrg_bal_peh, WindEurope outlook 2026–2030, DNV (solar outlook). Data as of October 2026.
